Your money, in seconds

Personal finance app for iPhone

Flux helps you see where your money went, how much you have saved and when your investments pay out. All in one place.

Your data never leaves your iPhone

Flux does not operate its own servers. Everything you log is stored on your own device. We cannot see your transactions.

What it does

  • Expenses: log them by hand, by voice, or let Flux pick them up from your bank's text messages.
  • Savings: set money aside in goals so you do not spend it without noticing.
  • Investments: track your term deposits and stocks, and never miss a maturity date or a dividend.
Flux is a personal tracking tool. It is not a bank or an investment platform: it moves no money, places no orders and gives no financial advice. Every decision about your money is yours.
