Frequently asked questions

Can Flux read my messages?

Only the text you decide to hand it. Flux has no access to your Messages app or your email: it cannot open them or search them. Automatic logging works through an automation in the Shortcuts app that you create: when a message from your bank arrives, that automation hands its text to Flux, which reads it inside your iPhone to get the amount and the merchant. You decide which messages trigger it, and you can turn it off whenever you want.

Is my financial data on a server somewhere?

Not on Flux servers: we don't have any. Your data is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, also in the private database of your iCloud account, on Apple's servers. Flux has no access to that information. Keep in mind that anyone who uses your same Apple ID on another device will be able to see that data in Flux.

From version 1.2, iCloud sync turns on by itself if your iPhone is signed in to iCloud. If you already used Flux and had data, nothing is uploaded until the app tells you once with an on-screen notice. If your App Store region is mainland China, Flux does not use iCloud and everything stays on your iPhone. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Automatic logging stopped picking up my purchases

This can happen if the bank changes the format of its messages or if the Shortcuts automation got turned off. Check in Shortcuts that it is still active. If the problem continues, write to us describing the format without including amounts, account numbers or personal data.

An expense was logged incorrectly

Tap the transaction on the Home screen to correct the amount, the description, the date or the category, or to delete it.

What plans are there, and how does the Pro trial work?

Flux offers monthly, six-month and yearly Pro plans. The plans start with a 2-week free trial, offered once per Apple account: if you cancel before it ends, you are not charged anything. Before you subscribe you will see the applicable price and billing cycle.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

From your Apple account settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Flux. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged anything; if you cancel later, you keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Plan changes take effect at the end of the current period.

How do I get a refund or withdraw from the purchase?

You have 5 business days from the first charge to withdraw, or the period set by your country's law if it is longer. Apple handles the charge, so Apple also handles the refund: request it at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you withdrew within the period and Apple does not grant it, write to us with the date of the charge: we file the claim with Apple on your behalf and tell you Apple's answer.

I want to file a complaint

Write to soporte.flux.app@gmail.com with your name, what happened and what you are asking for. We usually reply within 2 to 3 business days, and always within 15 business days at most. If you are in Colombia and are not satisfied, you may go to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce; if you live in another country, to your local consumer protection authority.

I changed iPhones — do I lose my data?

Not if you use iCloud: from version 1.2, sign in with the same Apple ID on the new iPhone, leave iCloud turned on for Flux and open the app. Your data arrives on its own; the first time it may take a few minutes, and you need a connection and available iCloud storage. While it arrives, the app may look empty or ask you for the initial setup: don't delete anything. If you don't use iCloud —or if your App Store region is mainland China—, your data moves to the new iPhone with a direct transfer from the old one or by restoring a backup —from iCloud or from your computer—. Language, currency and appearance are not synced: you choose them again.

How do I turn off iCloud for Flux?

In Settings → your name → iCloud, in the list of apps using iCloud, turn off Flux. From then on, what you log is stored only on that iPhone. What was already in your iCloud is not deleted by turning it off; this iPhone may stop showing it, but it stays in your account and comes back if you turn iCloud for Flux on again. If you want to delete it too, see the next question.

How do I erase all my data?

In the app: Profile → Your Data → Delete all my data. It can't be undone. If iCloud is on for Flux, the deletion also reaches your iCloud and your other devices with the same Apple ID when they connect to the internet; a device where you have turned off iCloud for Flux keeps its copy until you delete it there. If you already turned off iCloud for Flux, delete the copy in your iCloud from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Flux. Uninstalling the app does not delete what is in your iCloud, and it does not cancel your subscription. iPhone backups made earlier may keep that information until they are replaced.

How long do you keep my support message?

Messages sent to support are kept for up to 90 days to handle your case and are then deleted.
