Frequently asked questions

Can Flux read my messages?

No. Flux does not access your text messages directly. Automatic logging works through an automation in the Shortcuts app that you create: when a message from your bank arrives, that automation can hand text to Flux. You decide which messages trigger it, and you can turn it off whenever you want.

Is my financial data on a server somewhere?

Not on servers operated by Flux. Everything is stored on your iPhone.

Automatic logging stopped picking up my purchases

This can happen if the bank changes the format of its messages or if the Shortcuts automation got turned off. Check in Shortcuts that it is still active. If the problem continues, write to us describing the format without including amounts, account numbers or personal data.

An expense was logged incorrectly

Tap the transaction on the Home screen to correct the amount, the description, the date or the category, or to delete it.

What plans are there, and how does the Pro trial work?

Flux offers monthly, six-month and yearly Pro plans. The Pro trial lasts 1 month and is offered once per Apple account. Before you subscribe you will see the applicable price and billing cycle.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

From your Apple account settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel them. Cancellation and any plan change take effect at the end of the current period. Apple handles charges and refunds under its own policies.

I changed iPhones — do I lose my data?

Flux does not sync with iCloud yet, but your data moves to the new iPhone if you set it up with a direct transfer from the old one or by restoring a backup —from iCloud or from your computer—. If you set it up as a new iPhone, without a backup, the data stays only on the old one.

How do I erase all my data?

In the app: Profile → Your Data → Delete all my data. It is immediate and irreversible. iPhone backups made earlier may keep that information until they are replaced.

How long do you keep my support message?

Messages sent to support are kept for up to 90 days to handle your case and are then deleted.
