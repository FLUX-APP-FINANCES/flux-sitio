3. Accuracy of the information

Automatic logging depends on the format of your bank's messages, which can change without notice. Always check against your actual bank statements before making any decision.

Term deposit returns are estimates: they are calculated from the effective annual rate you enter, on a 365-day basis, assuming principal and interest are paid together at maturity. A term deposit usually cannot be withdrawn before maturity, so its "value today" is not money you can use. The value of your stocks is the one you report; Flux does not look up market prices.
