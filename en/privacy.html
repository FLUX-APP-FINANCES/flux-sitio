Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains how Flux handles your information. The app is developed and offered by Daniel Andrés Peláez Sierra, an individual domiciled in Bogotá D.C., Colombia, who acts as the data controller under Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (now compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and other applicable rules.

In short

Flux does not operate its own servers. The expenses, goals and investments you log are stored on your iPhone. We have no access to your financial data.

1. What information is stored, and where

The data you enter in the app —such as amounts, descriptions, categories, savings goals and investments— is stored on your device.

Flux does not sync with iCloud yet. If you have your iPhone's backup turned on —in iCloud or on your computer—, that backup includes Flux's data. Apple makes and stores it, under its own relationship with you and in accordance with its privacy policies; Flux cannot access it. If Flux offers iCloud sync in the future, we will update this policy before turning it on.

2. Text messages from your bank

If you choose to use automatic logging, the text of your bank's messages reaches Flux through an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app that you set up and can turn off whenever you want.

The text is analyzed inside your iPhone to extract the amount and the merchant. Flux does not send it to its own servers or to third parties; only the resulting transaction is kept, not the original text of the message.

Flux does not access your text messages directly. It only receives the text that the automation you created decides to hand it.

3. Voice logging

If you use voice logging, the audio is turned into text by speech recognition that runs inside your iPhone. If your device does not support that on-device recognition, Flux disables dictation rather than sending the audio to a server. Flux does not keep recordings.

The microphone and speech recognition require your explicit permission, which you can revoke from your iPhone's settings.

4. Analytics, diagnostics and advertising

Flux does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or diagnostic tools. The app does not send financial, usage or device data to its own servers or to analytics providers.

If you turn on Apple's Share with App Developers option in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may make crash reports available to the developer under its own policies. That service is Apple's, and you can turn it off from your iPhone's settings.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed through Apple's in-app purchases. Flux neither receives nor stores your card or payment details. Billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled under Apple's policies.

6. Email support

When you write to soporte.flux.app@gmail.com, we handle your email address and the information you choose to share —for example, your iPhone model, iOS version or a description of a problem— solely to respond to your request. Please avoid sending amounts, account numbers, full text messages or other sensitive financial information.

Support messages are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Email is managed through Gmail; Google handles the mailbox's information under its own policies.

7. Who your information is shared with

We do not sell, rent or transfer your financial data. It stays on your device. Apple is involved in payments and, if you have it turned on, in your iPhone's backup, under its own relationship with you. Google is involved only as the provider of the support email described above.

8. How long it is kept

Your financial data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app. You can erase it from Profile → Your Data → Delete all my data or by uninstalling the app. iPhone backups made before you erase it may keep that information until they are replaced or you delete them. Support messages are handled for the period stated in section 6.

9. Your rights

Under Law 1581 of 2012, you can access, update, correct and delete your personal data, withdraw your consent where applicable, and file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce (Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio), Colombia's data protection authority. People in other countries may have additional rights under their local laws.

Because your financial data is not in our systems, you can manage it directly from the app. For anything else —support messages, for example— you can exercise your rights as explained below.

10. How to submit an inquiry or a claim

Inquiries and claims are handled directly by the data controller, Daniel Andrés Peláez Sierra, at soporte.flux.app@gmail.com. It is the only channel, and it is free of charge. They may be submitted by the data subject, their successors, or a duly authorized representative.

  • Inquiries (finding out what information we hold about you): answered within 10 business days from the day we receive them. If that is not possible, we will tell you why and when we will answer, which will be no more than 5 additional business days.
  • Claims (correcting, updating or deleting information, withdrawing consent, or reporting a breach): include your name and ID, a description of the facts, the address or email where you want to receive the answer, and any documents you wish to rely on. Claims are answered within 15 business days from the day after they are received, extendable by up to 8 business days if we tell you why before the first deadline expires. If a claim is missing information, we will ask for it within 5 days; if two months pass without it, the claim will be considered withdrawn.

Before going to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, the law requires first completing this inquiry or claim process with the data controller.

11. Children

Flux is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Effective date and changes to this policy

This version of the policy is effective from 23 September 2026. Support messages are kept for the period stated in section 6; your financial data, for as long as you keep the app or until you erase it.

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the date in the header. When a change is significant, we will announce it inside the app where reasonably possible.
