Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Flux handles your information. The app is developed and offered by Daniel Andrés Peláez Sierra, an individual domiciled in Bogotá D.C., Colombia ("the developer"). The developer acts as the data controller for the personal data he receives directly —the messages you send to support (section 6)— under Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (now compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015) and other applicable rules. The financial data you log in Flux is kept by you, for your personal use, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account: the developer does not receive it, view it or have any access to it. This policy still explains what the app does with it and how you control it.

In short

Flux does not operate its own servers or user accounts. The expenses, goals and investments you log are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, also in your own iCloud account, a service provided by Apple. We have no access to your financial data.

1. What information is stored, and where

The data you enter in the app —such as amounts, descriptions, categories, savings goals, investments, month-end closings and the pending messages described in section 2— is stored on your device.

From version 1.2, if your iPhone is signed in to iCloud and you have not turned off iCloud for Flux, the app syncs that data with the private database of your iCloud account, so it shows up on your other devices with the same Apple ID. In that case it turns on by itself, without you having to switch it on, and you can turn it off in Settings → your name → iCloud. If you already used Flux before version 1.2 and had data saved, nothing is uploaded to your iCloud until the app tells you so, once, with an on-screen notice; if you install Flux for the first time, we tell you in the welcome screens.

That storage is provided by Apple under its iCloud terms and its privacy policy, uses space in your iCloud plan, and is on Apple servers that may be outside your country, including the United States. Apple encrypts that data; unless you turn on iCloud's Advanced Data Protection, Apple keeps the keys to that encryption, except for what is end-to-end encrypted (section 2). Flux has no access to that database: we cannot view, download or modify its contents. Anyone who uses your same Apple ID on another device will be able to see that data in Flux.

Mainland China: if your App Store account region is mainland China, Flux does not use iCloud and your data is stored only on your device. This does not apply to Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan.

If you don't use iCloud, your data is stored only on your device. If you turn off iCloud for Flux, what you log from then on is stored only on that device, and what was already in your iCloud stays there until you delete it (section 8). That device may stop showing what came from your iCloud; that copy stays in your account and comes back if you turn iCloud for Flux on again.

In addition, if you have your iPhone's backup turned on —in iCloud or on your computer—, that backup includes Flux's data. It is made and stored by Apple or by your computer, under your own relationship with them; Flux cannot access it.

2. Messages and emails from your bank

If you choose to use automatic logging, the text of your bank's text messages or emails reaches Flux through an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app that you set up and can turn off whenever you want.

The text is analyzed inside your iPhone to extract the amount and the merchant. Flux does not send it to its own servers. Once the transaction is logged, the transaction is kept, not the text of the message. If Flux can't fully understand it, or if your plan doesn't include automatic logging, the text is stored as a pending message along with the rest of your data —on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account (section 1)— until you complete or dismiss it, and at that point it is deleted. It is also deleted with “Delete all my data”.

When a pending message reaches your iCloud, its text and the bank's name are stored with end-to-end encryption: the keys are only on your devices, and neither Apple nor we can read them. The rest of your data in iCloud —for example, that message's amount and date, or the description of your transactions— uses the standard iCloud encryption described in section 1.

We recommend that the automation only runs for purchase and transfer messages, not for messages with passwords or security codes. If your bank sends those codes with the same opening as purchases, Flux does not log them as an expense: they stay among pending messages, on your phone and with the encryption described above, and you dismiss them with one tap.

Flux has no access to your Messages app or your email: it cannot open them or search them. It only reads the text that the automation you created decides to hand it.

3. Voice logging

If you use voice logging, the audio is turned into text by speech recognition that runs inside your iPhone. If your device does not support that on-device recognition, Flux disables dictation rather than sending the audio to a server. Flux does not keep recordings.

The microphone and speech recognition require your explicit permission, which you can revoke from your iPhone's settings.

4. Analytics, diagnostics and advertising

Flux does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or diagnostic tools. The app does not send financial, usage or device data to its own servers or to analytics providers.

If you turn on Apple's Share with App Developers option in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may make crash reports available to the developer under its own policies. That service is Apple's, and you can turn it off from your iPhone's settings.

Because Flux does not track you across websites or apps, and does not let third parties do so, it does not change its behavior in response to "Do Not Track" browser or system signals.

5. Payments and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed through Apple's in-app purchases. Flux neither receives nor stores your card or payment details. Charges, renewals and refunds are processed by Apple; how to cancel, the right of withdrawal and how to file a complaint are explained in the Terms of Use.

6. Email support

When you write to soporte.flux.app@gmail.com, we handle your email address and the information you choose to share —for example, your iPhone model, iOS version or a description of a problem— solely to respond to your request. Please avoid sending amounts, account numbers, full text messages or other sensitive financial information.

Support messages are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Email is managed through Gmail; Google handles the mailbox's information under its own policies.

We process those messages because you write to us and in order to answer you (your consent and handling your request). The mailbox is on Google's servers, which may be outside your country, including the United States.

7. Who your information is shared with

We do not sell, rent or transfer your financial data, and we do not share it with anyone: it stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Apple is involved in payments, in iCloud sync and in your iPhone's backup, as the provider of those services to you and under its own terms. Google is involved only as the provider of the support email described above.

8. How long it is kept, and how to erase it

Your financial data stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account, until you erase it. Profile → Your Data → Delete all my data removes it from that device and, if iCloud is on for Flux on that device, also from your iCloud and from your other devices with the same Apple ID when they connect. If you turned off iCloud for Flux, or switched Apple IDs, that button no longer reaches your iCloud: to delete the copy left there, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Flux and delete its data. Uninstalling the app only removes it from that device: if you use iCloud, your data stays in your account and comes back if you reinstall the app. iPhone backups made before you erase it may keep that information until they are replaced or you delete them. Support messages are handled for the period stated in section 6.

9. Your rights

Under Law 1581 of 2012, you can access, update, correct and delete your personal data, withdraw your consent where applicable, and file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce (Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio), Colombia's data protection authority.

Because Flux has no access to your financial data —neither on your iPhone nor in your iCloud—, you access, correct and erase it yourself from the app and from your iCloud settings (section 8): there is no copy of ours to request. For anything else —support messages, for example— you can exercise your rights as explained below.

If you live outside Colombia, you also have the rights your country's law gives you —for example, the LGPD in Brazil, Mexico's federal data protection law or Law 21.719 in Chile—, and we will handle them through the same channel and within the shortest deadline that law requires. You can file a complaint with your country's data protection authority.

10. How to submit an inquiry or a claim

Inquiries and claims are handled by the developer, Daniel Andrés Peláez Sierra, at soporte.flux.app@gmail.com. It is the only channel, and it is free of charge. They may be submitted by the data subject, their successors, or a duly authorized representative.

  • Inquiries (finding out what information we hold about you): answered within 10 business days from the day we receive them. If that is not possible, we will tell you why and when we will answer, which will be no more than 5 additional business days.
  • Claims (correcting, updating or deleting information, withdrawing consent, or reporting a breach): include your name and ID, a description of the facts, the address or email where you want to receive the answer, and any documents you wish to rely on. Claims are answered within 15 business days from the day after they are received, extendable by up to 8 business days if we tell you why before the first deadline expires. If a claim is missing information, we will ask for it within 5 days; if two months pass without it, the claim will be considered withdrawn.

Before going to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, the law requires first completing this inquiry or claim process with the data controller.

11. Children

Flux is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Effective date and changes to this policy

This version of the policy is effective from 13 November 2026. Version 1.2 of Flux adds the iCloud sync described in section 1. Support messages are kept for the period stated in section 6; your financial data, until you erase it, as explained in section 8.

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the date in the header. When a change is significant, we will announce it inside the app where reasonably possible.
